# Hostel Food Waste & Quality Analytics System - Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)

In this project, I analyzed hostel mess operations data to understand food consumption patterns, food waste generation, meal quality factors, and operational efficiency across multiple mess halls. The objective was to transform raw mess management data into actionable insights that help reduce food wastage, optimize food preparation, improve meal quality, and support data-driven decision-making in hostel food services.

The project was built using multiple interconnected datasets covering different aspects of hostel dining operations, attendance patterns, menu planning, weather conditions, student feedback, and food quality assessments. These datasets were integrated to create a unified analytics framework capable of identifying the key drivers behind food waste and student satisfaction.

**The dataset consisted of several interconnected tables covering:**

• Daily Mess Operations: Food prepared, consumed, wasted, meal types, menu items, serving sizes, preparation schedules, and operational details.  
• Student Attendance: Daily hostel attendance and meal participation records.  
• Food Quality Feedback: Student ratings and feedback on food quality, taste, hygiene, and service.  
• Menu & Nutrition Data: Cuisine categories, food types, protein levels, and special menu information.  
• Weather Data: Temperature, rainfall, humidity, and seasonal conditions.  
• Academic & Event Calendar: Examination periods, holidays, festivals, and special events affecting mess attendance.  
• Mess Infrastructure Data: Mess hall information, staff allocation, and operational resources.  

**Using Python (Pandas and NumPy), I performed extensive data cleaning, preprocessing, and validation activities, including:** 

• Handling missing values and inconsistent records  
• Standardizing categorical variables and time formats  
• Detecting and treating outliers and anomalies  
• Validating food balance relationships between preparation, consumption, and waste  
• Merging multiple datasets for integrated analysis  
• Creating derived metrics and business KPIs  

**After preparing the data, I conducted Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to uncover patterns and trends related to:**

• Food Waste Trends  
• Waste Percentage Analysis  
• Meal-wise Waste Patterns  
• Menu Item Performance  
• Attendance vs Consumption Relationships  
• Food Quality Impact on Waste Generation  
• Seasonal and Weather-Based Influences  
• Exam and Event Impact on Mess Operations  
• Cuisine and Food Type Preferences  
• Operational Efficiency Metrics  

**Using Matplotlib and Seaborn, I created a wide range of visualizations including:**

• Time Series Trend Analysis  
• Distribution and KDE Plots  
• Correlation Heatmaps  
• Boxplots for Outlier Detection  
• Category-wise Comparison Charts  
• Attendance and Consumption Analysis Visuals  
• Waste Contribution Analysis  
• Food Quality and Satisfaction Visualizations  

**Key metrics analyzed include:**

• Total Food Prepared (kg)  
• Total Food Consumed (kg)  
• Total Food Wasted (kg)  
• Waste Percentage (%)  
• Food Redistribution Quantity  
• Average Student Attendance  
• Meal Participation Rate  
• Food Quality Ratings  
• Cost of Food Waste  
• Menu Performance Metrics  

The final analysis provides actionable insights into the factors driving food waste and student satisfaction in hostel mess operations. By combining data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualization techniques, the project identifies opportunities to improve food planning, reduce wastage, optimize resource utilization, and enhance the overall dining experience for students.

**This project revealed key factors behind food waste and consumption patterns, enabling data-driven decision-making in hostel mess operations.**
